# FINAL COPY AUDIT — Desi Draft

Every visible string on the site was read in the rendered page at 1440 and 390, then tested
against seven questions: what it says, whether a visitor needs it, whether it is accurate,
whether it repeats something else on the page, whether it sounds like Desi Draft, whether it
could be said more clearly, and whether the page shows evidence for it.

Voice target: an independent, contemporary Indian studio. Confident, direct, no corporate
register, Desi cultural reference only where it does real work.

---

## 1. Home — hero

| Item | Verdict | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `we make content for the new desi mainstream` | The brand line. Says the category, the audience and the ambition in one breath. | Kept verbatim. Split into word masks for the reveal, but the sentence is untouched. |
| Sticker smiley / sparkle / ellipse | Decoration with a job: they mark the word the studio is named for. | Ellipse kept, underline removed (two annotations on one word was clutter). |

No hero subheadline was added. The line is the claim; the next section pays for it.

---

## 2. Home — intro

| Before | Problem | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Audiences are more scattered *and* more reachable than ever. We help brands become leaders on the channels of the new mainstream.” | Restates the hero (`new mainstream`), makes an unverifiable claim (`become leaders`), and says nothing about how the studio works. | “Every feed has its own rhythm. We plan, write, shoot and cut for the one your audience **actually** scrolls — with the same six people from first call to export.” |

The replacement answers the question the section raises (who are the people, what do they do)
and drops the second use of “new mainstream” on the same screen.

## 3. Home — manifesto

| Before | Problem | After |

The honesty line is now stated once, on `/work`, where a visitor is actually checking evidence.

## 9. What was deliberately not written

- No superlatives (“award-winning”, “world-class”), no client counts, no reach or revenue
  numbers, no testimonials, no “trusted by”.
- No Hindi or Hinglish sentence was inserted for flavour. The one Hinglish phrase on the site
  (`shaadi season = content season`) predates this pass and belongs to a portrait card.
- No SEO paragraphs or keyword stuffing were added to any section.

## 10. Outstanding copy gaps (blocked on real information)

1. The one-line studio description for meta/social previews is still generic because no
   approved positioning sentence exists beyond the hero line.
2. Case-study `outcome` paragraphs are absent for every project. Writing them would mean
   inventing results.
3. Team bios are absent for the same reason.
4. The **creative spaces** service group has no project to point at.
